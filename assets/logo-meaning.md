# ViPi logo

- **Red and gold**: the colour pair shared by the Vietnamese and Chinese flags — one palette for both languages ViPi types.
- **V–P ligature**: the V's right arm *is* the P's stem, so Vi(etnamese) and Pi(nyin) are literally one stroke — two input methods in a single tool.
- **Gold star**: the five-pointed star of both flags, nested in the V's opening as the mark's accent, like a diacritic sitting on a letter.
- **Form**: a macOS Big Sur–style rounded square; the 16 px size drops the star and thickens the strokes so the VP stays crisp in the menu bar and Finder.
