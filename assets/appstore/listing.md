Description:

```
ViPi is a keyboard for people who write in Vietnamese, Chinese and English every day.

VIETNAMESE AND ENGLISH, WITHOUT SWITCHING
Type Telex and ViPi tells the two languages apart as you go: treen becomes trên, and server stays server. Spell checking only adds marks where they make a real Vietnamese syllable, and words that are not Vietnamese go back to what you typed.

CHINESE PINYIN
Tap the language key for Pinyin, with a large word list and suggestions that follow what you have just typed. Choose Simplified or Traditional characters, turn on fuzzy Pinyin for sounds you mix up, and use full-width punctuation. Words you pick can come first next time.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
• Keys sized and placed like the system keyboard, including the full iPad layout with Tab and Caps Lock, and a number row on 13-inch iPads
• Hold a key on the top row for its number; on iPad, flick down for the second character
• A bar above the keys shows Chinese candidates, and for Vietnamese the word with marks or exactly the keys you typed; you can hide it while typing Vietnamese
• Light and dark appearance, and a split keyboard on iPad

YOUR TYPING STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
ViPi has no code that connects to the internet. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Full Access is optional and only used to vibrate on key presses. Learned words stay on your device.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
ViPi is free software under GPL-3.0. Read the code at github.com/t43dev/ViPi.

To start, open Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard and choose ViPi. Then hold the globe key in any app and pick ViPi.
```

```
ViPi 是为每天使用越南语、中文和英语的人设计的键盘。

越南语和英语，无需切换
直接用 Telex 输入，ViPi 会自动区分两种语言：treen 变成 trên，server 仍是 server。拼写检查只在能组成真实越南语音节时才加声调，不是越南语的词会恢复为原来输入的字母。

中文拼音
点按语言键即可输入拼音，词库丰富，并根据刚输入的内容给出建议。可选简体或繁体，为容易混淆的读音开启模糊拼音，并使用全角标点。选过的词下次可以优先显示。

为 iPhone 和 iPad 而设计
• 按键的大小和位置与系统键盘一致，包括带 Tab、大写锁定键的 iPad 完整键盘，以及 13 英寸 iPad 上的数字行
• 长按第一排按键输入数字；在 iPad 上向下轻扫输入按键上的第二个字符
• 按键上方的栏显示中文候选词；输入越南语时显示带声调的词或原样输入的字母，输入越南语时也可以隐藏此栏
• 浅色和深色外观，iPad 上可分离键盘

输入内容只留在设备上
ViPi 没有任何联网代码。没有账户、广告或统计。“完全访问”是可选项，仅用于按键振动。学习的词语只保存在您的设备上。

免费开源
ViPi 是遵循 GPL-3.0 的自由软件。源代码：github.com/t43dev/ViPi。

开始使用：打开“设置”>“通用”>“键盘”>“键盘”>“添加新键盘”，选择 ViPi。然后在任意应用中按住地球键并选择 ViPi。
```

## Notes for App Review

```
ViPi is a custom keyboard. To use it: Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > ViPi. Then tap or hold the globe key in any text field and choose ViPi, or open the ViPi app and use the "Try it here" field. No login is needed.

Full Access is optional and only used to vibrate on key presses; the keyboard types fully without it. The keyboard has no network code. Settings chosen in the app reach the keyboard through an App Group that the keyboard only reads.
```
