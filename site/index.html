    Questions

    ViPi suddenly stopped typing in every app

    Some app has turned on Secure Input: a password field, a terminal with Secure Keyboard Entry, or a password manager. The menu-bar icon shows a lock. Open Secure Input Monitor from the menu to see which app it is, then close that field or quit the app.

    An English word came out as Vietnamese

    Words that are also real Vietnamese syllables, such as of becoming ò, cannot be told apart automatically. Press the tone key twice (off gives of), or press Backspace right after an automatic change to get the word back as you typed it. To use Vietnamese and English separately, turn off VE in Settings, Vietnamese.

    Do I have to uninstall UniKey, EVKey or GoNhanh?

    No, just turn them off while you use ViPi. Two tools rewriting the same keystrokes fight each other.

    Which Macs are supported?

    macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

    Does ViPi send what I type anywhere?

    No. Everything happens on your Mac. The learned Pinyin words stay in a file on your Mac, and the only request ViPi makes is the update check to GitHub.

    No. Everything happens on your phone, and the learned Pinyin words stay there. ViPi uses the internet for one thing, the update check to GitHub. Turn off Install updates automatically in its Settings and it will not go online unless you tap Check for updates.

    No. Everything happens on your device, and the learned Pinyin words stay there.

    How do updates work?

    Once a day ViPi asks GitHub for a newer version, downloads it on Wi-Fi and checks that it carries the same signature as the app you have. Then it installs while the keyboard is closed. The first update needs your permission and one confirmation; after that Android usually installs them without asking.

    I have a Huawei phone. Which version do I need?

    HarmonyOS 4 and older run Android apps, so use the Android version. HarmonyOS 5 and later do not, so those phones need the HarmonyOS version, which has no download yet.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Choose Quit ViPi from its menu, then drag ViPi from Applications to the Trash.
