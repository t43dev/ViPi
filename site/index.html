Vietnamese and Chinese on one Mac keyboard.

ViPi puts Telex and Pinyin in one small menu-bar app. Press ⌃ ⇧ to change language. Free, open source, and it never goes online except to check for updates.

    Learns from you

    The words and phrases you pick come first next time. They are stored only on your Mac and clear with one click.

    Settings that fit on one screen

    • The interface speaks English, Tiếng Việt and 简体中文.
    • Light or dark, following macOS or your choice.
    • Updates itself: it checks GitHub, verifies the download is signed by the ViPi developer, and relaunches when you stop typing.
    • Starts at login and stays in the menu bar.

    What you will not find

    • No price. Free forever under GPL-3.0. There is no Pro version.
    • No tracking. Keystrokes are processed on your Mac and never leave it. The only network request is the update check to GitHub.
    • No ads, no account, no nagging.
    • No warnings. ViPi is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it straight away and the Accessibility permission survives updates.

    Set up in under a minute

    1. Download ViPi.dmg, open it and drag ViPi onto Applications.
    2. Open ViPi and allow it in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Accessibility. It starts working within two seconds.
    3. Press ⌃ ⇧ and type.

    Turn off other Vietnamese input methods such as UniKey, EVKey or GoNhanh, and keep the plain ABC input source, so only ViPi changes what you type.

    Questions

    ViPi suddenly stopped typing in every app

    Some app has turned on Secure Input: a password field, a terminal with Secure Keyboard Entry, or a password manager. The menu-bar icon shows a lock. Open Secure Input Monitor from the menu to see which app it is, then close that field or quit the app.

    An English word came out as Vietnamese

    Words that are also real Vietnamese syllables, such as of becoming ò, cannot be told apart automatically. Press the tone key twice (off gives of) or switch to English with ⌃ ⇧.

    Do I have to uninstall UniKey, EVKey or GoNhanh?

    No, just turn them off while you use ViPi. Two tools rewriting the same keystrokes fight each other.

    Which Macs are supported?

    macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

    Does ViPi send what I type anywhere?

    No. Everything happens on your Mac. The learned Pinyin words stay in a file on your Mac, and the only request ViPi makes is the update check to GitHub.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Choose Quit ViPi from its menu, then drag ViPi from Applications to the Trash.
