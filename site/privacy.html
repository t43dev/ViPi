ViPi

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

ViPi is a free, open-source keyboard for typing Vietnamese and Chinese. This policy covers the ViPi apps for iPhone and iPad, Android, macOS and HarmonyOS, and this website.

ViPi does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data. ViPi has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What you type

ViPi turns your keystrokes into text on your device. Your typing is never sent to us or to anyone else. On Android, in password and number fields, ViPi passes each key straight through without processing it or learning from it. On iPhone and iPad, the system replaces any third-party keyboard with its own in password fields.

What stays on your device

  • Learned words. When you pick a Chinese word, ViPi can remember it so that it comes first next time. You can turn this off or clear the list in Settings.
  • Clipboard history (Android). The keyboard keeps the last 6 items you copied for up to 1 hour so you can paste them again. You can remove items or clear the list from the clipboard panel.
  • Settings. Your choices, such as theme and layout.

This data stays in ViPi's private storage. On Android it is not included in cloud backups, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Internet access

  • Android, version 0.3.4 and later: ViPi does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. The links in its Settings screen open in your browser.
  • Android, version 0.3.3: ViPi contacts GitHub to check for and download updates. The request contains no typing or personal data, but GitHub receives your IP address as with any web request.
  • macOS: ViPi contacts GitHub to check for and download updates, in the same way.
  • HarmonyOS: ViPi does not have the internet permission.
  • iPhone and iPad: ViPi has no code that connects to the internet, with or without Full Access. The links in the app open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Vibrate (Android, HarmonyOS): for key press feedback, which you can turn off.
  • Full Access (iPhone and iPad): optional, and only used to vibrate on key presses. ViPi types fully without it. Turning it on does not send anything anywhere: ViPi has no network code.
  • Accessibility (macOS): required so ViPi can read keys and insert text in other apps. Keys are processed on your Mac and are not recorded.

This website

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it. The page asks GitHub for the latest version numbers from your browser. The site sets no cookies; it only remembers your light or dark choice in your browser.

Children

ViPi is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub. You can also read ViPi's source code to check how it works.
